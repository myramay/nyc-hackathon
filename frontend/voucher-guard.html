“No programs. Must earn 40x the rent.”Details 1BR · $2,200/mo · Crown Heights, Brooklyn · Craigslist · found Sep 26 DiscriminatoryRefuses vouchersIncome rule on full rent
What we found
  • “No programs” refuses tenants whose rent is paid by a voucher.
  • “Must earn 40x the rent” applies an income rule to the full rent instead of the tenant's share.
The math
  1. 40 × $2,200 = $88,000 a year required.
  2. A CityFHEPS tenant pays 30% of income: example household earning $24,000 pays $600/month; the voucher pays $1,600.
  3. Applied to the tenant's share: 40 × $600 = $24,000. The household qualifies.
  4. $88,000 is more than any CityFHEPS household can earn. The rule excludes every voucher holder.
