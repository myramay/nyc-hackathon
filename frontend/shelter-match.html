Text message · ready to send

Apply at Franklin Shelter, 1122 Franklin Ave, Bronx, or HELP Women's Center, 114 Snediker Ave, Brooklyn (both 24/7). Bring: any ID you have, a recent pay stub if you work, your medications. Denied? Legal Aid 800-649-9125. Directions: 311.

If you're denied

  • Ask for the decision in writing
